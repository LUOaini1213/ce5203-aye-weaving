KEY FINDINGS
====================================================================================================

1. RAMP METERING (RM) — only consistently beneficial strategy
   • Peak: avg delay -7.7%, capacity +11.6%, network timeloss -22.7%
   • Off-peak: marginal (-2.8% timeloss)

2. VSL (single-lane on E_weaving_0, 25→12 m/s @ occ>0.15)
   • Hurts in both periods: peak +20.9% timeloss, off-peak +88.2%
   • Reason: AYE bottleneck is lane-change-conflict-limited, not speed-limited.
     Lowering speed on lane 0 holds back exit-bound traffic and worsens upstream queue.

3. VSL + RM — worse than RM alone in both periods
   • The VSL component drags the combined result down by ≈40%.
   • Recommendation: deploy RM only; abandon VSL on this section.

4. OFF-PEAK is already near free-flow (53.5 km/h, 9.3 s/veh delay)
   • Any active control measure has limited upside, and VSL is actively harmful.
